What is Academic Verification?

Academic verification is the process of confirming a candidate's educational
qualifications directly from recognized institutions or trusted records. It helps
employers and organizations ensure that certificates, degrees, diplomas, and
academic claims are authentic and accurate.

Klinsheet helps simplify this process by providing a structured and secure verification service designed to support informed recruitment and compliance decisions.
