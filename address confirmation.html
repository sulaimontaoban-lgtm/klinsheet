What is Address Confirmation?

Address confirmation is the process of verifying a candidate's stated residential or business address through physical checks and trusted records. It helps employers and organizations confirm that applicants live where they claim and can be reliably contacted.

Klinsheet provides a secure and structured address verification service designed to support safer hiring, reduce fraud, and strengthen compliance decisions.
