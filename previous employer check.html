What is Previous Employee Check?

Previous employee check is the process of confirming a candidate's employment history directly with former employers. It helps organizations verify job titles, dates of employment, responsibilities, and professional conduct.

Klinsheet helps simplify this process with a structured and confidential verification service designed to support informed hiring and reduce CV fraud.
